## Problem Title
Reducing Agricultural Middlemen Through a Digital Auction and Smart Distribution Platform.


## Problem Creater's Organization
Smart India Hackathon (SIH).

## Theme
Agriculture, Smart Technology and Supply Chain Management.

## Problem Description
Farmers in India face several challenges while selling their agricultural products. They often depend on multiple intermediaries to transport and sell their crops in the market.

These intermediaries reduce farmers' profits by adding extra commissions and charges. At the same time, consumers pay higher prices for agricultural products.

Farmers also face difficulties in finding bulk buyers, understanding market demand and getting the correct price for their products.

Traditional selling methods do not always provide transparent pricing or direct communication between farmers and large-scale buyers.

A smart digital system is required to improve agricultural trading, reduce unnecessary commissions and provide better market opportunities for farmers.




## Proposed Solution
We propose KISAN SETU, a digital agricultural auction and distribution platform that helps farmers sell their products through a transparent online bidding system.

Instead of depending on several intermediaries, farmers can list their crops and receive price offers from registered buyers such as retailers, wholesalers, restaurants and food processing companies.

Key Features

1. Digital Crop Auction: Farmers can list their products, and registered buyers can submit competitive price bids.

2. Farmer Group Formation: Small farmers in nearby areas can join together and sell their products in bulk.

3. Live Market Price Updates: Farmers can view current market prices and make better selling decisions.

4. Bulk Buyer Connection: The platform connects farmers with wholesalers, retailers and food industries.

5. Quality Grading: Products can be categorized based on quality, quantity and grade.

6. Transport Pooling: Farmers can share transportation services to reduce delivery expenses.

7. Auction Notifications: Farmers receive notifications when buyers place new bids.

8. Digital Sales Records: Farmers can view their previous sales, accepted bids and earnings.

9. Demand Information: Buyers can share their product requirements so farmers can understand market demand.

6. Technical Solution

KISAN SETU will be developed as a web-based application using frontend and backend technologies.

Technologies Used

- Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
- Backend: Python Django Framework.
- Database: MySQL.
- UI Framework: Bootstrap.
- Charts: Chart.js.
- Notifications: Email notifications.

System Working Process

1. Farmers and buyers register on the platform.
2. Farmers upload crop details, quantity, quality and expected price.
3. Buyers browse available agricultural products.
4. Buyers submit competitive price bids.
5. Farmers compare the received bids.
6. Farmers select the most suitable offer.
7. The system generates an order confirmation.
8. Farmers can arrange shared transportation for delivery.
9. Sales details are stored in the database.

System Architecture

Farmer / Buyer Registration
↓
Frontend – HTML, CSS, JavaScript
↓
Django Backend
↓
Auction Management and Price Comparison
↓
Buyer Matching and Order Processing
↓
MySQL Database
↓
Bid Confirmation and Sales Report


## Feasibility and Viability
Technical Feasibility

The system can be developed using Python Django, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and MySQL. These technologies are widely available and suitable for web application development.

Economic Feasibility

The platform can use open-source technologies, reducing development expenses. Shared transportation and bulk selling can help reduce additional costs.

Operational Feasibility

The application will provide a simple interface for farmers and buyers. Basic digital knowledge is sufficient to use its main features.

Business Viability

The platform can connect individual farmers, farmer groups, wholesalers, retailers and food processing industries.

It can be expanded to different agricultural markets and regions.
